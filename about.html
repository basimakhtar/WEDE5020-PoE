About VoltStream Plumbing & Electrical

VoltStream Plumbing & Electrical (Pty) Ltd is a family-owned services company based in Johannesburg, South Africa. Since 2011 we have completed more than 6,000 plumbing and electrical jobs for homeowners, body corporates and small businesses across Gauteng.

Our Story

VoltStream was founded in 2011 by master plumber Zanele Mthembu as a one-van plumbing service operating from Sandton. Word-of-mouth referrals grew the business quickly, and in 2016 we added a full electrical division after customers repeatedly asked for a contractor they could trust with both trades.

In 2020 we opened a second branch in Randburg, and in 2023 we launched our 24/7 emergency response line. Today a team of 14 licensed plumbers and electricians serves more than 900 households every year.

Our Mission

To keep South African homes and businesses safe, powered and flowing by delivering dependable plumbing and electrical services with honesty, speed and transparent pricing.

Our Vision

To become Gauteng's most trusted plumbing and electrical services provider - the first number people save for every water or power emergency.

  • Safety first: Every job complies with South African building and wiring regulations.
  • Honesty: We quote before we work and never invent problems to inflate the bill.
  • Respect: We treat your home like our own - dust sheets down, shoes off, mess cleaned.
  • Craftsmanship: We are registered artisans who take pride in work that lasts.
