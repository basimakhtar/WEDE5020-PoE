## Image preparation (free online tools)

All photographs were downloaded at 1600px wide and then optimised for the web:
- resized to a maximum width of 1400px;
- re-compressed to JPEG quality 72 (Squoosh was used as the reference tool; see README references).

## Sources evaluated but not used

| Source | Why evaluated | Why not used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Openverse (openverse.org) | Aggregates CC-licensed images with creator/licence metadata | Flickr-sourced photos were low resolution or visually dated |
| Wikimedia Commons | Public domain and CC images with full attribution | Relevant results were archival/historical scans, not suitable for a modern business site |
| Pexels / Pixabay | Free stock photo libraries | Direct downloads were blocked (403 hotlink protection) from the build environment |

## Text content

All text content on the website is **original copy written for this project** about the fictional organisations VoltStream Plumbing & Electrical and Paws of Hope Animal Shelter. Facts used as inspiration (e.g., South African wiring regulations, PIRB plumbing registration) were checked against:
- Plumbing Industry Registration Board (PIRB). 2026. Available at: https://pirb.co.za [Accessed 1 September 2026].
- Department of Employment and Labour (RSA). 2026. *Electrical Installation Regulations*. Available at: https://www.labour.gov.za [Accessed 1 September 2026].
