Image Preparation

The photographs were downloaded at 1600 pixels wide and then prepared for the web by resizing them down to a maximum of 1400 pixels and re-compressing them to JPEG quality 72. Squoosh was the reference tool for this workflow, and the same optimisation was applied locally. This keeps the pages fast without ruining the photos.


Sources Researched but Not Used

Openverse was the first alternative looked at, because it collects Creative Commons images and shows the creator and licence for each one. The Flickr results it returned were mostly low resolution or looked dated, so they were not used.

Wikimedia Commons was checked next. It has plenty of public domain and CC images with full attribution, but the relevant search results were old archival scans, which do not suit a modern business website.

Pexels and Pixabay were also tried, but direct downloads were blocked from the build environment, so nothing was taken from them.


Text Content

All the writing on the website is original copy written for this project, about the fictional organisations VoltStream Plumbing and Electrical and Paws of Hope Animal Shelter. The factual bits used as inspiration, like the South African wiring regulations and PIRB plumbing registration, were checked against these sources:
